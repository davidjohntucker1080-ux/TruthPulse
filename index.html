Can a camera catch a lie that people miss?

Tell is a two-player game and a real experiment. One person answers questions after being secretly told to lie or tell the truth. The other person guesses. Tell measures tiny face movements and learns, game after game, whether lies leave a trace.

Your data

Tell never saves video or sound. It saves face-movement numbers on this phone only, and nothing is uploaded. To combine games from other phones, export the data on each phone and import the files here.
